AI won't eliminate code review. It will change what it's for.

When an AI agent can generate hundreds of lines in minutes, reading every line becomes a poor use of senior engineering time. Define the expected behavior first, automate the checks you can prove, and save human review for architecture, trade-offs and failure modes. The goal isn't less accountability. It's better accountability.
